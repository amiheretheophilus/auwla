# Triaxis: Developer Build Brief

Nov 16, 2026  · @Ezekiel

Triaxis is a subscription app for trade signals in forex, crypto and synthetics (the "3 axes"). **The app is the product, not a Telegram bot.** Members sign up, subscribe, read signals and, for crypto, connect an exchange. Professionals use a private admin panel to enter trade ideas that an AI has already reviewed. The app checks the numbers, draws a branded score card, and shows it only to members who paid for that axis. For crypto, it can also open the trade on each member's futures account at the risk they chose. Members subscribe to one axis or all three, and affiliates earn from the members they refer. Telegram is an optional alert channel and is not part of the core build.

## 1. How a signal travels

Every signal follows the same path. Build the app around this one flow.

1. **Analyst enters the idea** in the admin panel: axis, asset, direction, entry, stop loss, take-profit targets, and the result of the AI review they ran on another platform. A risk percentage is optional draft data.
2. **The app checks it.** It checks price order and how far the target is from entry compared with the stop. Three further checks come from the AI review (stop placement, trend, news). Any fail means rejected. Any "not sure" means it cannot be posted.
3. **The app draws the score card** (a 1080 x 1080 image in the Triaxis brand) for that axis.
4. **The card is published** to members who hold that axis, in the app feed, with a push notification and an email alert (Telegram alerts are optional).
5. **Crypto only: the trade is opened** on every connected member's futures account, sized to that member's chosen risk %.
6. **Results follow.** The analyst marks TP1, TP2, TP3, stop loss or break-even. A result card is posted as a reply, and open trades are updated (stop moved to entry after TP1).

Rule for the whole product: nothing reaches members unless it has passed the checks. There is no override button.

## 2. Admin panel (the signal desk)

A private web page for approved analysts only. It works like a charting site's symbol box, but limited to the three axes.

**Pick the asset.** Choose an axis, then search or browse groups. Forex: majors, minors, metals (gold, silver). Crypto: USDT perpetual futures, and any new coin that exists on the exchange. Synthetics: Volatility 10 to 100 (also the 1-second versions), Boom and Crash

**Fields the analyst fills in:**

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Axis and asset | Chosen from the list, not free text |
| Direction | Buy or Sell. Show Long or Short for crypto |
| Entry type | Market, limit or stop. Only market entries are auto-traded in version 1 |
| Entry, stop loss, take-profit targets | Enter prices individually or paste them in that order. Analysts can add and remove targets |
| Risk (%) | Optional draft data. Accept any non-negative percentage without a warning or cap. Members choose their own risk |
| AI review: platform used | Required. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or other. Saved with the signal |
| AI review: three answers | Stop placement, trend alignment, news window. Each is Pass, Fail or Not sure |
| Optional | Chart screenshot, news calendar text, a short note |

**Rules the panel enforces:**

- Levels must be in the right order (for a buy: stop below entry, targets rising). Otherwise reject.
- The distance from entry to TP2 must be at least 1.5 times the distance from entry to the stop. At twice the stop distance it is a full pass. With a single target, compare TP1 instead.
- Any Fail means the idea is rejected and no levels are shared. Any Not sure blocks posting.
- Show a preview of the exact card before the Post button. A double click on Post must not post twice.
- Log who posted what and when. Signal IDs run TRX-0001, TRX-0002 and are never reused.

**Honesty requirement.** Members are told the three judgment checks come from an AI review. The panel therefore forces the analyst to name the AI platform used, and stores it. Do not build a way to skip that step.

## 3. The signal score card

The card is the product's face. It is generated by the app, never drawn by hand. Size is 1080 x 1080 pixels, PNG. Dark background, a faint repeating TRIAXIS watermark, and the same layout on every axis so members recognise it at a glance.

**Every card shows:**

- TRIAXIS logo and name at the top left, and an axis tag at the top right
- Asset name in large type, a one-line description, and a Buy/Sell (Long/Short) badge
- Entry, stop loss, and every take-profit target, with its distance from entry relative to the stop distance
- The price order, target distance, stop placement, trend alignment, and news window checks
- Signal ID (TRX-0143), date and time in GMT
- A seal reading OFFICIAL TRIAXIS SIGNAL, and a one-line "not financial advice" notice

**Three axis variants** (same layout, different accent colour and tag):

| Axis | Tag | Accent colour | Example asset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forex | AXIS 1 · FOREX | Blue #3987e5 | XAUUSD, EURUSD |
| Crypto | AXIS 2 · CRYPTO | Purple #9085e9 | BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT |
| Synthetics | AXIS 3 · SYNTHETICS | Amber #eda100 | VOL 75, BOOM 1000 |

**Other cards the system must produce:**

- **Result cards** for TP1 hit, TP2 hit, TP3 hit, stop loss hit, and closed at entry. They show the entry and targets as a progress line and a "next step" line.
- **Rejected card**, shown to the analyst only. It lists which checks failed and shares no price levels.
- **Draft preview**, shown to the analyst before posting, marked DRAFT.

**How to build it:** write the card as an HTML template, fill it with the signal's numbers, and render it to PNG with a headless browser (Playwright or Puppeteer). The working templates already exist in the prototype (see section 8), including fonts: Unbounded for headings, Instrument Sans for text, IBM Plex Mono for numbers. Escape all text that goes into the HTML.

Also export a portrait version (1080 x 1920) later for WhatsApp status and stories.

## 4. Crypto auto-trading on futures

When a crypto signal is posted, the app also opens the trade on every member who has connected an exchange and switched auto-trading on. Forex and synthetics are signal-only, because those brokers have no shared trading API.

**Exchanges for version 1:** Binance USDM futures, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, KuCoin futures. Use the ccxt library, which gives one interface for all of them.

**Connecting an exchange (member side):**

1. Member creates an API key on their exchange with trading permission only. Withdrawals must be off. The key should be locked to the server's IP address.
2. Member pastes the key and secret (plus a passphrase for OKX, Bitget, KuCoin). The fields are masked, sent once over HTTPS, and never shown again.
3. The app checks the key. Where the exchange allows it (Binance, Bybit), it reads the key's permissions and refuses any key that can withdraw. For the others, it tells the member to confirm this by hand.
4. Keys are stored encrypted (Fernet or a cloud key vault), shown only masked, and deletable by the member at any time.
5. Member accepts a plain-language risk agreement before auto-trading can be switched on. Save the time of acceptance.

**Risk setting.** Each member picks a risk per trade from 0.1% to 50% of their account. Above 5% they must tick an extra warning (the "unlock"). The app then picks the leverage automatically. The member never types a leverage.

**How the app sizes each trade:**

- Position size = account balance x risk % ÷ (distance from entry to stop + fees + slippage). So if the stop is hit, the loss equals the chosen risk.
- Margin budget = 50% of the account for one trade, so one signal cannot use the whole account.
- Leverage = the lowest whole number that fits the position inside the margin budget.
- Safety cap: liquidation price must sit beyond the stop by at least 1.5 times the stop distance. If the leverage needed breaks that rule, or exceeds the exchange maximum, the position is made smaller and the member is told the real risk.
- Margin mode is isolated, so one trade cannot drain the rest of the account.
- Skip the trade if the market price has already moved more than 30% of the stop distance away from the posted entry, or is already past the stop or TP1.

**Worked example.** Account $1,000, risk 1% ($10), entry 68,400, stop 68,058 (a 342-point stop, 0.5%). Position is about $2,000 of Bitcoin. The 50% margin budget is $500, so the app picks 4x leverage. Fees make the position slightly smaller.

Now the same account at 50% risk ($500) with the same stop. That would need a $100,000 position, far beyond the margin budget. The app shrinks it to the most the limits allow (about $25,000 at 50x), which risks about 12.5%, and tells the member so.

**Orders placed for each trade:** a market entry, a reduce-only stop loss, and three reduce-only take-profit orders splitting the position 40% / 30% / 30%. If the stop or take-profit orders fail to place after the entry fills, the app must close the position at once. A trade must never be left without a stop.

**After TP1:** when the analyst marks TP1, move every open trade's stop to the entry price. Place the new stop first, then cancel the old one, so there is never a moment with no stop.

**Controls the owner needs:**

- Modes: paper (simulated, the default), testnet (exchange practice accounts), live. Live needs a deliberate switch.
- A global halt button that stops all new auto-trades instantly. Open trades keep their stops and targets.
- Per-member on/off, and a trade log showing size, leverage, effective risk and result.
- Every member gets a push notification and an in-app entry for each trade opened, skipped (with the reason) or failed.

**Rules for access:**

- A member can hold several subscriptions at once (for example forex for 1 month and crypto for 3 months). Access to an axis is allowed if any active subscription covers it.
- Buying again for the same axis adds time on top of the current expiry. It does not restart the clock.
- Admins never expire. Revoking a member removes their access and switches off their auto-trading.
- When access ends, the member loses the axis in the app and their auto-trading on it stops. Remind them 3 days before expiry.
- Upgrading from one axis to all three needs a price rule (see open decisions).

**Enforcement.** Every request for a signal, card or trade setting checks the member's active subscriptions on the server, never only in the screen. A locked axis shows an upgrade button. When access ends, that axis locks and the member's auto-trading on it stops. Remind members 3 days before expiry by email and push notification. **Optional** **Telegram:** if the owner later wants Telegram alerts, use one private channel per axis with single-use invite links that expire within an hour, and automatic removal at expiry. Treat it as an add-on.

**Payments.** Version 1: the member pays by bank, mobile money or USDT, submits a reference, and an admin taps Approve or Reject. A payment can be decided once only. Version 2: automatic payment confirmation through a payment provider's webhook, so no admin tap is needed.

## 6. Affiliate links

Every affiliate gets a unique referral link. When someone joins and pays through it, the affiliate earns a commission. This is new work: the prototype has no affiliate feature.

**How it works:**

1. An approved affiliate gets a unique code, for example KOJO24, and a link (triaxis.app/r/KOJO24), which opens the app if it is installed, or the store page with the code carried through (a deep link).
2. A visitor who opens the link is tagged with that code. Remember it for 30 days (suggested default) using a cookie on the web, or the deep link's code on mobile. Let new members also type a code by hand at sign-up as a fallback.
3. The first time that person signs up, the app permanently links them to the affiliate. Later links from other affiliates do not replace it.
4. When an admin approves that member's payment (or the payment provider confirms it), the app adds a commission to the affiliate's balance.
5. After a hold period (suggested 14 days, to cover refunds and disputes), the commission becomes payable.
6. The owner pays affiliates on a schedule (for example monthly, in USDT) and marks the commission as paid.

**Affiliate dashboard shows:** their links with a copy button, clicks, sign-ups, paying members, commission pending, commission payable, commission paid, and a payout history. Affiliates see counts and earnings only. They must never see a referred member's name, contact or exchange details.

**Rules and safeguards:**

- **One level only.** An affiliate earns on members they refer directly, never on people those members bring in. Multi-level payouts look like a pyramid scheme and create legal risk.
- No commission on a self-referral (same email or phone, same payment reference or the same device).
- No commission on refunded or reversed payments. Reverse any commission already added.
- Commission is earned on paid subscriptions only, never on sign-ups or free trials.
- An admin can freeze or remove an affiliate, and see an audit trail of every click, sign-up and commission.

## 9. Security and legal must-haves

This product holds members' exchange keys and can trade their money. A mistake here costs members real money and exposes the owner.

**Security (the developer must build these):**

- Encrypt exchange keys and secrets at rest. Never write them to logs. Show only a masked version. Back up the encryption key separately.
- Refuse any exchange key that can withdraw, wherever the exchange lets the app check. Tell the member to switch withdrawals off everywhere else.
- Every trade and every post is idempotent: a retry or a double click must never place two orders or post twice.
- Never leave an open position without a stop. If protection orders fail, close the position and alert the member.
- Role-based access for the admin panel (analyst, admin, affiliate manager), two-factor login for staff, and an audit log of every post, grant, revoke and halt.
- A global halt switch, and alerts to the owner when trades fail, an exchange errors, or the AI review is missing.
- Daily backups of the database, and a tested restore.

- Rate limits and input validation on every endpoint. Escape every piece of text placed in cards and messages.
- Keep secrets (API tokens, encryption key, database password) out of the code, in environment settings.

**Legal and honesty (the owner must handle these):**

- Holding members' API keys and trading their accounts may count as regulated activity, such as investment advice or managed accounts, depending on the country. Get legal advice for every country with members before turning auto-trading on for the public.
- Every card and page carries a risk warning and states that signals are information, not financial advice. Never promise or imply guaranteed profits.
- The checks are labelled "AI-reviewed" only when an AI review was really run. The platform used is saved with each signal.
- Do not describe the product as a fully automated bot. It is analyst signals, AI-reviewed, with optional auto-trading.
- Publish the true track record, losses included.
- Collect and store member data according to the data protection laws of the members' countries, and provide a way to delete an account and its keys.
- Affiliates follow the code of conduct in section 6.
